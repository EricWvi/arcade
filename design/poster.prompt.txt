Create a large rectangular poster for a classic Flash game hosted on "Arcade", a website styled like the Apple App Store web version. The poster is the full-width background of the "Recently played" hero banner at the top of the homepage (about 1380×480 px on a desktop screen, wider on large screens). When the visitor hovers a game in that banner, the background switches to this poster, and the website overlays white text in the top-left area and a row of small square game tiles along the bottom.

Canvas and shape:
- Ultra-wide landscape, 21:9 aspect ratio, 2560×1097 px (if that size is unavailable, render 1536×1024 and keep all important content within the central horizontal band so it can be cropped to 21:9).
- The artwork fills the entire canvas edge to edge (full bleed), with four sharp 90-degree corners.
- Do NOT round the corners, no border, no frame, no vignette frame, no letterboxing, no white or transparent margins. The website applies its own corner rounding.

Composition (safe areas for website overlays):
- Place the main character(s) and focal action in the right half to right two-thirds of the canvas.
- Keep the top-left area (about 45% of width × 50% of height) relatively calm and mid-to-dark in tone, such as sky, mist or soft background scenery, so overlaid white headline text stays readable.
- Keep the bottom 30% of the canvas low in detail and darker, because a dark gradient and a row of game tiles will be overlaid there.
- The image must still work when the left and right edges are cropped by about 10% each on narrower screens.
- Cinematic, epic, wide establishing shot with strong depth and dramatic lighting.
- No text, no logo, no title, no letters, no UI, no HUD, no watermark.

Style:
- Premium cinematic key art, like a featured hero banner on the App Store or a game's launch poster.
- Faithful to the original game's art style, characters, world and color palette (pixel art stays pixel art, cartoon stays cartoon), refined and re-rendered at high resolution.
- Rich, saturated colors with atmospheric lighting.
