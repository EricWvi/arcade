Create a card cover image for a classic Flash game hosted on "Arcade", a website styled like the Apple App Store web version. The cover is the large landscape thumbnail at the top of a game card in a horizontal category shelf, displayed at about 655×410 px, with the game icon, title, subtitle and a button rendered by the website below it.

Canvas and shape:
- Landscape, 16:10 aspect ratio, 1600×1000 px (if that size is unavailable, render 1536×960).
- The artwork fills the entire canvas edge to edge (full bleed), with four sharp 90-degree corners.
- Do NOT round the corners, no border, no frame, no drop shadow, no letterboxing, no white or transparent margins. The website applies its own corner rounding.

Composition:
- A vivid key-art scene that shows what playing the game feels like: the main character(s) in action within a recognisable environment from the game.
- Keep the main subject within the central 80% of the canvas; important details must not touch the edges.
- Keep the bottom-right corner (about 25% of width × 20% of height) visually calm, because the website may overlay small round video controls there.
- Clear depth with foreground, subject and background; dynamic but readable at thumbnail size.
- The game's logo or title may appear once as a stylised wordmark in the upper or central area, integrated into the art; no other text, no UI, no HUD, no buttons, no watermark.

Style:
- Polished, premium promotional key art, like a featured game banner on the App Store.
- Faithful to the original game's art style, characters and color palette (pixel art stays pixel art, cartoon stays cartoon), refined and re-rendered at high resolution with rich lighting.
- Bright, saturated, inviting; must look good on both white (#ffffff) and near-black (#1c1c1e) page backgrounds.
