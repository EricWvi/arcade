Create a game icon for a classic Flash game hosted on "Arcade", a website styled like the Apple App Store web version. The icon is shown at about 128×128 px next to the game's title in store listings, so it must read clearly at small sizes.

Canvas and shape:
- Square, 1:1 aspect ratio, 1024×1024 px.
- The artwork fills the entire canvas edge to edge (full bleed), with four sharp 90-degree corners.
- Do NOT round the corners, do NOT draw a rounded-square or circular icon mask, no border, no frame, no drop shadow, no transparent or white margins. The website applies its own corner rounding.

Composition:
- One clear focal subject: the game's main character, mascot or most iconic object, large and centered, filling about 70–85% of the canvas.
- Simple, uncluttered background (a solid color, a soft gradient or a lightly textured scene) that contrasts strongly with the subject.
- Bold silhouette, strong shapes, high contrast, saturated but harmonious colors, so it stays recognisable when shrunk to 64 px.
- No text, no letters, no logo wordmark, no numbers, no UI elements, no watermark.

Style:
- Polished, premium mobile-game icon quality, like a top App Store game icon.
- Faithful to the original game's art style, characters and color palette (pixel art stays pixel art, cartoon stays cartoon), refined and re-rendered at high resolution.
- Must look good on both white (#ffffff) and near-black (#1c1c1e) page backgrounds.
